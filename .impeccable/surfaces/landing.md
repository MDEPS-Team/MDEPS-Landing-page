# Surface brief: landing (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Primary action: "Start now" → Session_registration.html. Secondary: Login → app.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Vantage means vantage point. The page is an observatory looking at your portfolio: projects are bodies in a live 3D orrery whose orbit arc is progress and whose light is health. It refuses the split hero with a flat dashboard screenshot and a row of equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Celestial-atlas plates: deep Prussian blue ground (brand #005B9A family, night #031A30), engraved white hairlines, coordinate graticule, fine orbit rings, small spaced-caps plate captions in mono for data. Status is light: on-track mint #7FE0C9, at-risk amber #FFB547, delayed coral #FF7A66 (4 sample projects, matching the assistant board). Light theme = the same plate printed blue ink on pale paper-blue. Display: Bricolage Grotesque; body Geist; data Geist Mono.

STORY: Visitor sees their portfolio from above, understands one place shows every project's health, watches the AI assistant answer from that data, sees how fast onboarding is, checks honest pricing, signs up.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 5/12: H1 (value prop, unchanged) at clamp(2.6rem, 6vw, 5.25rem), subheading, primary "Start now" + secondary "See the platform". Right 7/12: 3D orrery canvas (portfolio core + 5 project bodies on tilted rings, hover/tap reveals project card), tilts with pointer and device gyroscope, rotates with scroll. Legend of the three light states under it. Mobile: copy first, orrery below at 90vw square.

FORM: Observatory / celestial atlas orrery, position 5 of grounded list (assigned). seed:44240f50854f97cd. Raises: split-flap (portfolio board rows flip per character, columns fixed); seed packet (module cards turn in 3D: promise front, how-it-works back); labanotation (forecast drawn to exact scale along a time axis on scroll); rain-night (status as light, not badges).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
